Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your personal data and account information from ForkJury, please send an email to info@forkjury.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

We will process your request and permanently delete your data, including any information obtained through connected platforms such as Google, Facebook, or Yelp, within 30 days of receiving your request.

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us at the email above.
